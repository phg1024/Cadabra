Cadabra 是怎么做出来的How Cadabra was built

每个版本背后的故事，写在我的博客上：做了什么、试过什么没成、学到了什么。文章是英文的。 The story behind each release, from my blog: what changed, what I tried that didn't work, and what I learned.

  • 1.42026-11-19

    Hold a Key and Talk to Jarvis

    识别更偏向你自己的词，能用语音改写选中的文字，边说边打字；再加一个 Agent 键，通过插件把话交给 agent。Cadabra 只负责听，其余都归插件，而且在你自己装上并选中插件之前，什么都不会离开这台 Mac。 Recognition that leans toward your own words, rewriting a selection by voice, and typing as you talk; then an agent key that hands what you say to an agent through a plugin. Cadabra only listens and the plugin does the rest, and nothing leaves the Mac until you install and choose one.

    My Dictation App Now Fixes What It Mishears
